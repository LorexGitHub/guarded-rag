# PTO FAQ (draft)

This draft answers common questions about paid time off. It was written before the 2025 policy was final and has not been reviewed by People Operations, so some numbers may be out of date. Treat the PTO Policy as the source of truth.

## How many days do I get?

The draft assumed 22 days per calendar year for full-time employees, with up to four days carried over. Check the current policy for the final numbers.

## How do I ask for time off?

Use the people portal and give your manager a few days of notice. Your manager answers quickly in most cases, but long breaks around the end of a quarter are harder to approve.

## What about public holidays and company closures?

Public holidays are free. The winter closure is free as well. If you work a public holiday, you may take a day in lieu later.
