# Expense Policy

This policy tells employees what Acme Example pays for when they work, travel or entertain customers, and how to claim the money back. Spend company money as if it were your own, choose reasonable options, and keep every receipt. Claims that break these rules may be declined, and repeated misuse is handled under the code of conduct.

## What is covered

The company pays for transport to approved business trips, hotels up to 160 euros per night, meals while travelling up to 45 euros per day, and software that your manager approved in advance. Alcohol is not covered unless a customer dinner was approved beforehand. Personal items, fines and upgrades are never covered.

## Booking and approval

Book flights and hotels through the company travel tool so that invoices go straight to Finance. Trips that cost more than 1,000 euros need written approval from your manager before you book. Expenses above 5,000 euros in a single claim also need the approval of a department head.

## Filing a claim

File the claim in the expense tool within thirty days of the purchase. Attach a photograph or scan of the receipt, name the project or cost centre, and add one line about the business purpose. Claims without receipts are paid only up to 25 euros and only once per quarter. Finance pays approved claims with the next monthly payroll.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel often may receive a corporate card. The card is for business costs only. Reconcile every card transaction in the expense tool by the fifth working day of the next month. Lost cards must be blocked at once, then reported to Finance.

## Mileage, per diems and foreign currency

Private car use for business is reimbursed at 0.30 euros per kilometre, with the route recorded in the claim. Meals on days with an overnight stay use the per diem rate instead of receipts. Claims in a foreign currency are converted at the rate on the day of purchase, and the card statement serves as proof of that rate. Tips are reimbursed up to ten percent where they are customary. Gifts for customers must stay below 50 euros per person and need a note naming the recipient's company.
