# Code of Conduct

Acme Example expects every employee, contractor and visitor to treat others with respect. This code describes the behaviour we expect and what happens when someone falls short. It applies in the office, in video calls, in chat, at events and on customer sites, wherever you act as a representative of the company.

## Respect and inclusion

Treat colleagues, customers and partners fairly and politely. Harassment, discrimination, bullying and insulting jokes have no place here, whether they happen in person or online. Listen to different views, give feedback about the work and not the person, and assume good intent until you learn otherwise.

## Honesty and conflicts of interest

Be truthful in reports, timesheets and expense claims. Tell your manager early when you have a personal or financial interest that could affect a business decision, for example a family member who works for a supplier. Do not accept gifts worth more than 50 euros from anyone who does business with the company.

## Protecting information

Customer data, employee records and unreleased plans are confidential. Share them only with people who need them for their work, and only through approved tools. Do not copy personal data into private notes, public chat rooms or tools that Acme Example has not approved. Report any suspected data leak to the security team immediately.

## Social media and public speaking

Make clear that your views are your own when you write about work in public. Do not post screenshots of internal tools, customer messages or unreleased features. If a journalist or conference organiser contacts you about the company, send the request to the communications team before you reply. Speaking at events on your own time is welcome, and the company will help you prepare slides and review them for confidential content.

## Raising a concern

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, you can speak to your manager, to Grace Park at grace.park@example.com, or to Samir Haddad at samir.haddad@example.com for matters that involve security or data. You may also report a concern anonymously through the ethics form in the people portal. The company does not allow retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Consequences

People Operations looks into every report promptly and fairly. Depending on the findings, the outcome ranges from a coaching conversation to a written warning or the end of employment. Serious matters, such as threats or fraud, may also be passed to the authorities.
