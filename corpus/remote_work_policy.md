# Remote Work Policy

Acme Example supports remote work because focused work and flexible hours help many teams do their best work. This policy sets the shared rules so that remote work stays fair for people in the office and people at home. It applies to all employees whose role allows remote work. Roles that need physical presence, such as the office manager, follow the schedule in their offer letter.

## Eligibility and agreements

After the first ninety days, employees may work remotely up to three days per week with their manager's agreement. Fully remote arrangements need a written agreement signed by the manager and People Operations. The agreement names the working country, because tax and employment rules differ between countries. Work from another country for more than fourteen days in a year needs advance approval.

## Core hours and availability

Every team agrees on four core hours in which everyone is reachable by chat and video. Outside core hours, employees may shape their day as they like, as long as deadlines are met. Keep your calendar current and mark focus time so colleagues know when you are away. Update your chat status when you step out for more than an hour.

## Equipment and security

Acme Example provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset for the home office. Use the company laptop for all work, and keep its disk encryption and automatic updates on. Do not work on customer data in public places where others can see your screen, and use the company VPN on any network you do not control. Report a lost or stolen device to the security team within one hour.

## Home office allowance

New remote employees receive a one-time allowance of 400 euros for a desk, a chair or lighting. Keep the receipts and file them under the expense policy. The office manager, Ruth Mbeki, arranges shipping of company equipment and can be reached at ruth.mbeki@example.com.

## Working across time zones

Remote employees who live more than three hours away from their team agree on a shared overlap with that team. Meetings are scheduled inside the overlap, and long discussions are written down so that people in other zones can reply later. Record decisions in the team wiki. Travel to the office for planning weeks is paid by the company and is announced at least six weeks ahead, so that employees can arrange childcare or other commitments around it.

## Questions

Raise questions about eligibility or agreements with April Winter at april.winter@example.com. Employees who disagree with a decision may ask for a second review by the head of People Operations within ten working days.
